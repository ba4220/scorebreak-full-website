The Technology

What is Airborne?

Airborne is ScoreBreak's local network video replay system - purpose-built hardware and software bringing instant replay to any sideline, anywhere.

It runs entirely on its own local wireless network - no internet, no external server latency, just fast, reliable replay when every second matters.

No internet required - runs on its own local network
Integrated live stats feed directly in the replay interface
Works at any venue - from practice fields to major stadiums
Deeply connected to ScoreBreak coaching software
