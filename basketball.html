Film Analysis

A comprehensive set of tools built for efficiency and customization.

ScoreBreak's film analysis platform is built to fit your system. Coaches customize their own tagging workflows, organize film the way their staff thinks, and access everything from Mac, iPad, and iPhone.

Custom tagging workflows — build a system around your terminology, sets, and coaching style, and tag live, online or offline
Advanced analytics — multi-tag plays to generate tendency reports, location data, and shot charts automatically
Player and team film sessions — build personal breakdowns for individual athletes or full staff game plans with clips, notes, and drawings
Instant clip sharing — get film to players the moment you see something worth coaching, from within the app
AI Reporting — visual reports and stats generated from your tagged film, tailored to your data and what you want to see
