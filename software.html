Team Software

A Complete
Film Solution

ScoreBreak is film analysis software built with an obsessive focus on ease of use. Tagging, playback, sharing, and collaboration are designed to be intuitive from day one, so coaches spend less time navigating software and more time coaching.

Contact Us Learn More ↓

Analysis made simple

Cut, organize, and share film with your staff and players, from Mac, iPad, iPhone, or the web.

Interactive Stats

Tag live stats during games with no custom setup - the interface is ready in every account.

Tag Boards

Each coach builds a personalized button layout for the plays they care about - set up once, reuse all season. Droppable Pins for one-tap tags; Recordable Clips for variable-length cuts.

Playlists

Mix clips, title cards, whiteboard drawings, and images into one presentation. Build scouting reports, player review playlists, and team prep - share via chat or public link.

AI Analysis

ScoreBreak automatically analyzes uploaded film to generate location charts, stats, and play tags - no manual input. Just upload, and AI does the rest.

Data Reporting

An interactive matrix of statistics and tagged data built from your film. Every data point links directly back to the clip that created it — go from a number in a report to the play on the field in one click.

Remote Film Sessions

Run collaborative film sessions from anywhere - coaches and players watch and discuss film together in real time. Pause, mark up, and give feedback as a group from any device.

Unlimited Storage

Never delete a game again. Store every practice, scrimmage, and game with no storage limits - your entire film library stays accessible and searchable, forever.

Team Communication

Keep your program connected with built-in messaging - share clips, notes, and feedback directly within the platform. No third-party tools needed to stay in sync with players and staff.

Premium Service

ScoreBreak Handoff

ScoreBreak Handoff™ provides professional ready-to-use film breakdowns with full stats. When you can't find the time to break down film yourself, we do it for you - complete with tagged game film, breakdowns, and playlists. Every game is delivered within 24 hours by professional human analysts who have broken down thousands of lacrosse games since 2022.

Additional statistics - including shot charts and dodge reports - can be added to any Handoff package.
