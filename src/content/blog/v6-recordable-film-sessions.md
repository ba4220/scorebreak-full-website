Today, we're introducing version 6 of ScoreBreak for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS. The marquee feature of this version, Recordable Film Sessions, will revolutionize the way coaches create video analysis materials for preparing and developing their athletes. Our new recordable film sessions feature enables coaches to easily create video presentations with their voiceover and live-animated drawings, which athletes can review at their convenience.

**Takeaways:** ScoreBreak's new recordable film sessions feature allows for greater flexibility and convenience in creating and reviewing video analysis content. The ability to assign work ahead of time and record sessions allows for deeper discussions and more detailed feedback, leading to better understanding and execution of plays and strategies.

We understand that film sessions are a crucial aspect of sports team preparation, but it can be challenging to get everyone in the same room at the same time. With our new feature, coaches can now record their in-person film sessions in the app. All video playback interactions are recorded, along with the coach's voiceover, digital zooming and panning, and telestration drawings. The coach may then share the recordings with the team, enabling athletes in attendance to revisit the content at their convenience, or allowing members that were unable to attend the film meeting to gain access to the information they missed.

Our recordable film sessions feature is designed to help teams improve their performance and achieve their goals. The ability to distribute a pre-created voiceover ahead of an in-person film session allows for deeper discussions by allowing athletes to gain a base layer of understanding for the principals which will be reviewed in film. Coaches may record their insights and analysis while at their desk. The interface includes the ability to pause your recording, allowing the user to follow a natural workflow and record segments at a time.
