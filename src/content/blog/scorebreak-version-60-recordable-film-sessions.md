## Sports Tech Startup Launches Version 6 of iOS iPadOS macOS Software with Revolutionary Film Session Recording Feature

**Denver, Colorado** — ScoreBreak, a leading sports technology startup, has announced the release of version 6 of its iOS, iPadOS, and macOS software, featuring an innovative recordable film session feature that allows coaches to create video presentations with voiceover and live-animation drawings for athletes to review at their convenience.

This new feature will revolutionize the way coaches and athletes think about film sessions, as for the first time film sessions do not need to be done with a live audience. The feature may be activated during traditional in-person film sessions, and can also be used outside the film room — enabling what can be considered the "academic model" with coaches performing film review at their desk, assigning distributed video work ahead of time to their athletes. This approach enables deeper discussions when doing follow-on in-person film sessions.

<blockquote>"We are excited to launch version 6 of our software with the revolutionary film session recording feature," said Jordan Hendry, CEO of ScoreBreak. "We have seen in private testing with premier athletic teams that this feature will make a huge difference in the way coaches and athletes collaborate and communicate."</blockquote>

The new software is available now for download on the App Store for iPad and Mac and on the Mac App Store for macOS, and may be viewed online via the Web App.
