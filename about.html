Our Story

Built for the Coaches Who Demand More.

ScoreBreak is the sports technology company connecting athletes with coaches, giving teams the tools to outperform on film and on the field, and serving leagues and professional organizations across multiple sports.

Our Founding

Founded from Inside the Locker Room Out.

Jordan Hendry
Founder & CEO

ScoreBreak was founded in 2015 by Jordan Hendry - a retired professional lacrosse player for the Denver Outlaws and former collegiate athlete at the University of Denver. Jordan built ScoreBreak because he lived the problem firsthand: coaches needed better tools, faster film, and real intelligence on the sideline and in the film room.

What started as a solution for lacrosse has grown into a complete sports technology platform serving programs across lacrosse, basketball, and football.

Our Mission

Professional tools. Built for everyone.

ScoreBreak brings professional-grade technology that any coach, at any level, can pick up and use. From a first-year high school coach to a Division 1 program with a full staff, ScoreBreak meets you where you are. We believe every coaching staff deserves access to the same quality of tools that the pros use.
